Project

PocketSmart – Build Better Financial Habits

Overview

PocketSmart brings mindful spending, personal reflection, and goal setting together to help you build a healthier relationship with money. Understand your habits, make intentional choices, and connect everyday decisions with your longer-term priorities.

Features

  • Reflect on purchases: Recognise your spending habits and become more intentional with your money.
  • Set meaningful goals: Create savings targets and track your progress.
  • Understand your spending: Track income and expenses, manage your budget, and review spending patterns.
  • Build better habits: Align everyday choices with your priorities, one decision at a time.
