Research Project - Geometric Brownian Motion in the Indian Stock Market

Jun 2025 – Aug 2025

Conducted independent quantitative research evaluating the applicability of the Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM) model to the Indian stock market. Analyzed real-time data from eight major indices (including NIFTY 50, NIFTY 500, and NIFTY Realty) to assess short-term drift behavior. Results demonstrated high predictive alignment in broad-market and realty indices, while identifying structural breakdowns in Auto and IT sectors due to geopolitical and supply-chain disruptions. The research emphasizes GBM’s strengths in stable regimes and underscores the necessity of multi-factor models in non-stationary market environments.
