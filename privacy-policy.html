PocketSmart Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This policy describes how PocketSmart handles information in the PocketSmart mobile application.

PocketSmart helps users plan spending, track expenses, and work toward savings goals. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use PocketSmart, we may collect:

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number, and account identifier.
  • Authentication information: your email and password are processed by Firebase Authentication. PocketSmart does not store or display your password in plain text.
  • Financial-planning information: allowance or budget amounts, spending frequency, balances, savings goals, target amounts, priorities, progress, expense amounts, merchant or expense names, and categories that you choose to enter.
  • Technical information: information that may be generated during normal operation of the app, such as device, network, IP-address, diagnostic, and security information collected by our service providers.

PocketSmart does not ask for precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or bank-account credentials in the current version. Please do not enter full payment-card numbers, bank passwords, government IDs, or other highly sensitive information into free-text fields.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and secure your account;
  • save and display your budgets, expenses, goals, and progress;
  • calculate balances, savings projections, and goal progress;
  • provide support and respond to requests;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the app; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Services we use

PocketSmart uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to provide sign-in and cloud data storage. Firebase may process information on our behalf, subject to its own terms and privacy practices. You can read Google’s privacy information at policies.google.com/privacy.

Some goal illustrations may be loaded from third-party image hosts, including Unsplash. Those services may receive normal technical connection information, such as your IP address. Their own policies apply to those requests.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your financial planning data for advertising. We may share information only with:

  • service providers that host, secure, or operate PocketSmart;
  • professional advisers or authorities when legally required; or
  • a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain account and user-entered data while your account is active or as needed to provide the app. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us. Some information may remain temporarily in encrypted backups or where retention is required by law.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No internet transmission or electronic storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before completing it.

8. Children’s privacy

PocketSmart is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If the app will be marketed to children or families, the app owner must update this policy and implement any additional consent and child-safety requirements before distributing it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, its services, or applicable law changes. We will post the updated version on this page and update the “Last updated” date.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, email pcktsmart@gmail.com.

PocketSmart is a planning and tracking tool, not a bank, financial adviser, lender, or investment service.
