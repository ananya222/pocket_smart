GBM in the Indian stock market

ResearchFinancial Mathematics

Independent research June–August 2025

Overview

An evaluation of Geometric Brownian Motion across eight Indian indices, including NIFTY 50, NIFTY 500, and NIFTY Realty. The work compares short-term model behavior across broad-market and sector-specific indices.

The analysis identified stronger alignment in broad-market and realty indices, alongside breakdowns in Auto and IT during disruptive conditions. Those limitations led into the Extended Essay’s investigation of alternative models.
