PocketSmart – Build Better Financial Habits

Overview

With PocketSmart, my aim is to help teenagers track allowances, reflect on spending, and set savings goals. To encourage more deliberate financial decisions, I designed allowance-tracking and automated savings features.

Features

  • Reflect on purchases: My purchase-reflection tools help users recognise their spending habits.
  • Set meaningful goals: My goal-setting tools support savings targets and progress tracking.
  • Understand your spending: My app brings together income and expense tracking, budgeting, and spending reviews.
  • Build better habits: With these features, my aim is to connect everyday financial choices with longer-term priorities.
