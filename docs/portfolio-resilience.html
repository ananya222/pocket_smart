Research Paper - A Quantitative Framework for Portfolio Resilience: Insights from Indian Markets during COVID-19 and Demonetization

Jun 2025 – Aug 2025

Mentorship: Dr. Hesam Oveys, New York University professor

Organization: Pioneer Research

I engineered a generalized "Resilience Index"—integrating Volatility, Value at Risk (VaR), and Correlation Metrics—to quantify portfolio stability during "Black Swan" events where traditional metrics often fail. By applying this framework to the 2016 Indian Demonetization and the 2020 COVID-19 market shocks, I demonstrated that resilience-weighted portfolios consistently outperform traditional Sharpe-ratio-optimized models in risk-adjusted returns. My research concluded with the successful validation of this model’s predictive power across various historical disruptions, providing a robust framework for asset selection in high-volatility environments. My work has been accepted for publication to the International Journal of High School Research (IJHSR).
My research ranked in the top 5% of global scholars and was evaluated at an undergraduate honors thesis level.
