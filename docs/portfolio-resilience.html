Research

A quantitative framework for portfolio resilience

ResearchFinancial Mathematics

Pioneer Academics June–August 2025 Mentor · Dr. Hesam Oveys, NYU

Overview

Indian markets during COVID-19 and demonetization

How should a portfolio be evaluated when a major shock changes the relationships between its assets? This project developed a Resilience Index combining volatility, Value at Risk, and correlation metrics.

The framework was applied to the 2016 demonetization and 2020 COVID-19 disruptions in Indian markets, with comparisons against portfolios optimized using the Sharpe ratio.

Research outcomeAccepted for publication in IJHSRTop 5% evaluation · Pioneer Academics

Methods and outcome

The analysis brings together three views of risk: the size of fluctuations, exposure to losses, and the extent to which assets move together. It uses historical disruption periods to examine whether those measures offer a useful account of portfolio stability.

The paper has been accepted for publication in the International Journal of High School Research. The Pioneer program evaluated it in its top 5%, at undergraduate honors-thesis level. Supporting portfolio and market-analysis notebooks are available in the repository.
