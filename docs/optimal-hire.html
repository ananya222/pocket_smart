AI-Driven Hiring

Jun 2024 – Dec 2024

Organization: Candor International School

Developing a specialized matching engine using Siamese Neural Networks to align aspiring quantitative finance candidates with high-frequency and algorithmic trading roles. By leveraging Natural Language Processing (NLP) and shared-weight architectures, the system generates semantic embeddings for both resumes and job descriptions. The model calculates a similarity metric (Euclidean distance/Cosine similarity) to identify optimal "profile-to-role" fits, outperforming traditional keyword-based filters by recognizing the latent mathematical and programming competencies required in the quant space.
