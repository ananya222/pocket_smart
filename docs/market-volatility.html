Research

Indian market volatility & risk transmission

ResearchMarket Risk

Faculty-mentored research July 2026–present Mentor · Prof. Vineet Virmani · IIM Ahmedabad

Overview

Investigating the drivers of India VIX and the transmission of risk into Indian financial markets. The project combines market variables recorded each day with macroeconomic indicators published monthly or quarterly.

Research approach

A central challenge is aligning information recorded at different frequencies. The framework accounts for publication lags, so a model is not given information that would have been unavailable at the time it is predicting.

The work examines both short-term volatility dynamics and longer-term macro-financial influences, including spillovers from global markets. This is independent research under faculty mentorship.
