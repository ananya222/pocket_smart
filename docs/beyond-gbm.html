Beyond Geometric Brownian Motion: Modeling Fat Tails and Volatility in Financial Markets

Jun 2025 – Present

Organization: Candor International School

For my IBDP Mathematics Extended Essay, I am building on my earlier research to examine the mathematical foundations and limitations of Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM). My work focuses on identifying the specific failure points of the log-normal distribution assumption, particularly its inability to account for "fat tails" and volatility clustering. To address these gaps, I am exploring the Merton jump-diffusion model for discrete price shocks and the Heston model for stochastic volatility. Through this research, my aim is to develop a more robust comparative framework for modeling non-random market anomalies in emerging economies while deepening my understanding of stochastic calculus.
