Beyond Geometric Brownian Motion: Modeling Fat Tails and Volatility in Financial Markets

Jun 2025 – Present

Organization: Candor International School

Building upon my research on Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM), I am currently conducting a deep-dive analysis into the mathematical underpinnings and limitations of the GBM framework for my Math Extended Essay (IBDP). My work focuses on identifying the specific failure points of the log-normal distribution assumption, particularly its inability to account for "fat tails" and volatility clustering. To address these gaps, I am exploring alternative frameworks including the Merton Jump Diffusion model—to capture discrete price shocks—and the Heston Model, to incorporate stochastic volatility. This research aims to develop a more robust comparative framework for modeling non-random market anomalies in emerging economies while grounding me in stochastic calculus.
