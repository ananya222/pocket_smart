Faculty-Mentored Research – Indian Market Volatility & Risk Transmission

Jul 2026 – Present

Mentorship: Prof. Vineet Virmani, Associate Professor

Organization: Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad (IIMA)

I am conducting independent research under the mentorship of Prof. Vineet Virmani, Associate Professor in the Finance & Accounting Area at the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad (IIMA), investigating the determinants and transmission of volatility across Indian financial markets. I am developing a multi-frequency framework to model India VIX, integrating daily market variables with monthly and quarterly macroeconomic indicators while accounting for publication lags to avoid look-ahead bias. My research examines both short-term volatility dynamics and longer-term macro-financial drivers, with a broader focus on understanding risk spillovers from global markets into Indian financial markets.
