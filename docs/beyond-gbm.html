IBDP Mathematics Extended Essay

Overview

Modeling fat tails and volatility in financial markets

My Extended Essay investigates where the assumptions of Geometric Brownian Motion break down, particularly around extreme returns and volatility clustering.

Models under comparison

The Merton Jump Diffusion model introduces discrete price shocks. The Heston model allows volatility itself to vary stochastically. Comparing these frameworks with GBM provides a way to examine which market behaviors each model can capture.

The project builds on my earlier work with Indian indices and develops the mathematical argument behind the models, alongside their practical limitations.
