Research Project - Geometric Brownian Motion in the Indian Stock Market

Jun 2025 – Aug 2025

My independent quantitative research explored the applicability of Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM) to the Indian stock market. To assess short-term drift behavior, I analyzed real-time data from eight major indices (including NIFTY 50, NIFTY 500, and NIFTY Realty). My results demonstrated high predictive alignment in broad-market and realty indices, while identifying structural breakdowns in Auto and IT sectors due to geopolitical and supply-chain disruptions. My research emphasizes GBM’s strengths in stable regimes and underscores the necessity of multi-factor models in non-stationary market environments.
