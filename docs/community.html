Project Flow

Digital Media Coordinator Head

As Digital Media Coordinator Head at Project Flow, I work toward making conversations about menstrual health more open and welcoming. I want women and girls to feel comfortable sharing their experiences and asking questions without shame or judgement.

Project Flow is a student-led initiative at Candor International School that brings together reliable information on menstrual health, hygiene, and adolescent well-being. Our team makes these resources accessible to students, educators, and families, with a focus on dignity and reducing stigma.

Our work includes menstrual-hygiene education and a safe pad-disposal demonstration at Jyothi Orphanage, confidence-building activities at Doddabelle Government School, and a physical and mental well-being session at Hulimangala Government School. Through collective fundraising, the team raised over ₹16,000 for period kits containing biodegradable sanitary pads and disposal bags.

The team also publishes articles and research on access to menstrual-hygiene resources, religion and menstrual-health stigma, menstrual waste management in Bengaluru, and fatigue and burnout among Generation Z.
